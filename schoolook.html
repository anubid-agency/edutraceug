About Our School

Aligned with the New Ugandan Secondary Curriculum

Apex Model High School is fully certified by the Ministry of Education and Sports (MoES) and NCDC to deliver the Competency-Based Curriculum for Lower Secondary Education (Senior 1 through Senior 4).

Our educational approach moves away from rote learning, placing emphasis on self-directed inquiry, practical skill acquisition, teamwork, and values-driven leadership.
