Both scoring modes are built in. Switch any time.

Weighted assessments

Ideal for O-Level and schools using weighted continuous assessment. Give each assessment a weight and Edutrace computes the term total.

  • BOT Beginning of Term
  • CA Continuous Assessment
  • AOI Activity of integration
  • COURSEWORK Projects
  • MIDTERM and ENDTERM

Competency-based levels

Matches the NCDC competency-based curriculum. No percentages or averages, just clear levels for what each learner has achieved.

  • Level 1 Beginning
  • Level 2 Developing
  • Level 3 Achieved
  • Levels roll up per subject
  • Custom levels supported
